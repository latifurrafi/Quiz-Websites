01 — Who we are

The Daffodil AI Club is where CSE students go from reading about artificial intelligence to actually shipping it — models, demos, papers and projects you can put your name on.

This quiz is how we say hello. It is not an exam and nothing rides on your score. We just want to know where you are starting from, so we can point you at the right people and the right projects.

Workshops Project teams Research reading Competitions Mentorship

Everyone starts somewhere. Some of our strongest members walked in knowing nothing but curiosity — what mattered was that they kept showing up.

Answer honestly, take your guess when you are unsure, and enjoy it.
